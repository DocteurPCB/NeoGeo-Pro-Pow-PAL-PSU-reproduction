Reproduction of the NeoGeo Pro Pow PAL PSU - Voltek 33234-2

This is a reproduction of the Voltek 33234-2 used in the NeoGeo Pro Pow PAL PSUs. I started this project as my original PSU was heavily damaged, burnt and with many broken traces. This PSU is also subject to leaky capacitors causing shorts due to electrolyte.

After many repairs I decided to build a new one from scratch and here is the result. The repro works flawlessly and outputs a solid +5 volts. However, I will offer no support on this project.
